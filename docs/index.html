How it works in short

The GEXP week starts every Tuesday 12:00 AM Eastern Time, which is when the purge happens. Everyone needs the weekly requirement (55,000 GEXP by default; people who joined during the week need a proportional part). Players who can't reach it can ask for a breaklist or an excuse with the buttons on the reminders.

The breaklist keeps a player's days in the guild while they are away. Days come back (and keep growing) when they return; they are lost if the player joins another guild that the staff did not approve.

Times are shown in Eastern or Mountain Time depending on /timezone. Most staff commands reply with an image card.

Breaklist & excuses

/breaklist

The breaklist is for players who leave the guild for a while but keep their days and their place. These commands add, edit and remove entries, manage the days in guild and the GEXP debt, and change how the notice looks.

/breaklist add ign [reason] [start] [end] [gexp] [days] [debt] [guild] [purpose] [expected_return] [image1] [image2] [image3] staff

Add a player to the breaklist and post the notice (guild staff)

Adds the player and posts the notice card.

The bot reads the join date and the GEXP from Hypixel, works out how much GEXP should have been earned so far this week, and saves the difference as GEXP owed.

Enter the numbers by hand: the days in guild, the GEXP of that week and the old debt. Do it once for every player who is already on the breaklist, so that their days are saved.

Staff. Shows the card only to you first, with Confirm / Edit / Edit debt / Cancel. Nothing is posted until Confirm. Up to 3 optional images are posted after the cards. `gexp` is required when the bot has no saved GEXP for the player.

OptionWhat it does
ignrequiredMinecraft username of the player
reasonoptionalWhy the player is being breaklisted (everyone can read it)
startoptionalWhen they joined the guild (ET or MT, see /timezone): 2026/10/03 or 2026/10/03 6:00 PM (the year is optional: 10/03 6:00 PM). Default: from Hypixel
endoptionalWhen they were breaklisted (ET or MT, see /timezone). Default: now
gexpoptionalGEXP earned this week before the breaklist. REQUIRED when the bot has no saved GEXP for them (new member)
daysoptionalTotal days the player has spent in the guild (use it for players breaklisted before the bot)
debtoptionalExtra GEXP debt from previous weeks. Default: the saved debt
guildoptionalApproved guild they may join or visit (empty = they must stay guildless). Several: XL, Other
purposeoptionalWhy they visit that guild Choices: Temporary: can't meet our GEXP, Trying guilds to pick one
expected_returnoptionalDate they are expected back (no time): 2026/11/15 or 11/15
image1optionalOptional image posted after the breaklist cards
image2optionalOptional second image
image3optionalOptional third image
